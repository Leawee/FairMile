FairMile Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

FairMile is a mileage and expense log for people who drive their own car for work. This policy describes the iOS app. The English text is the policy.

Who we are

FairMile is published by an individual developer. The App Store listing shows the developer’s legal name. There is no FairMile account and no FairMile-operated server.

Information the app handles

  • Location while you record a trip. FairMile uses your location to calculate the driven path. Location is used only while you are recording a trip in this version. It is not used in the background, and the app does not request Always location permission.
  • Trip details you enter. Dates, places, miles, vehicle, category, purpose, notes, and whether you edited a record.
  • Receipt photos. Parking, toll, and other receipt photos you attach. The camera is used only when you take a photo.
  • Purchases. Apple processes subscriptions and the lifetime purchase. FairMile does not receive your card number.

Where it stays

Trip data, routes, and receipt photos stay on your iPhone by default. If you turn on the private iCloud copy, that data syncs through Apple’s private iCloud database for your Apple ID. FairMile does not upload mileage, routes, or photos to a server that the developer operates.

FairMile does not sell personal information, does not use third-party advertising or analytics, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Why location is used

FairMile uses your location while you record a trip to calculate mileage. Location is not used in the background in this version. You can add a trip by hand without granting location access. If you deny location, recording a path will not work until you allow it in Settings.

How long it stays

The log remains on your device until you delete a trip or delete the app. Deleted trips stay in Recently Deleted until you delete them permanently. Turning off iCloud does not by itself delete a copy already stored in your iCloud account; you can remove that from iCloud settings.

Children

FairMile is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through GitHub issues. Support is at leawee.github.io/FairMile/support.html.
