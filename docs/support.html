FairMile Support

Mileage log for taxes and reimbursement. Not tax advice.

FairMile records a drive while the app is open, lets you edit the trip, applies the IRS standard mileage rate for the day the trip started, and exports a PDF or CSV. It does not track in the background, and it does not file your taxes.

Contact

Write to the developer through GitHub issues. Include the iPhone model, iOS version, and what you expected to happen.

Purchases

FairMile Plus is sold by Apple. To restore a purchase, open Settings and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID that paid. To cancel a subscription, use the Apple subscriptions page: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Yearly is $39.99 and includes a 7-day free trial. Monthly is $4.99. Lifetime is $79.99 once. The free log allows 15 trips in each calendar month and a last-30-days export.

Location

FairMile uses your location while you record a trip to calculate mileage. Location is not used in the background in this version. You can add a past trip by hand if you do not allow location.
